slay-push is maintained on a volunteer basis. If it's useful to you or your company,
[sponsoring on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/firemanx07) helps fund the time that goes
into ongoing maintenance, security fixes, and new features.

{{< hextra/hero-button text="Sponsor on GitHub" link="https://github.com/sponsors/firemanx07" >}}

Sponsorship isn't required to use, fork, or deploy slay-push commercially — the
[Apache 2.0 license](../license) already permits all of that. It's simply a way to support the
project if you'd like to.
